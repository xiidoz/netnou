# Code of conduct

This project adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies to everyone who takes part in the project's spaces: issues, pull
requests, discussions and any other place where the project is represented.

In short:

- Be respectful and considerate. Assume good faith and criticise ideas, not
  people.
- Harassment, insults, discriminatory remarks and unwelcome personal
  attention are not acceptable.
- Respect people's privacy. Do not publish anyone's private information.
- Accept that maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that break
  these rules, and may exclude people who keep breaking them.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behaviour that breaks this code, report it
privately to the maintainers through
<https://github.com/xiidoz/netnou/security/advisories/new> and mark it as a
conduct report. The form is visible only to the maintainers. Reports are
handled confidentially.
